How the score works

TRANSPARENT

We compare skill phrases found in your resume against skills found in the job description. The score is the percentage of detected job skills that also appear in your resume. It is a useful signal, not a hiring prediction.

Privacy by design
Analysis runs locally in your browser. This app does not upload or save your resume text.
Contextual AI is optional
For richer feedback, connect an LLM through a backend you control. Never put a private API key in this page’s JavaScript.
